Privacy policy

This app is used only by its owner, George Wallace, to access his own Google Drive with rclone.

  1. Data accessed: files and file metadata in the owner's own Google Drive, through the Google Drive API.
  2. Use: copying and syncing the owner's files between his Google Drive and his own computers.
  3. Storage: the sign-in token and any copied files stay on the owner's own computers. Nothing is sent to any other server.
  4. Sharing: no data is shared with, sold to, or disclosed to anyone.
  5. Google user data use complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  6. Removal: the owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
